Account

Guest or logged in — never blocked

Checkout works with just a name and phone. Logging in (email OTP, password, Google, or Apple on iOS) prefills details and syncs order history across devices. Guest orders on this device attach to the account after login.

Payment truth lives on the server

The confirmation screen only appears after Razorpay reports success; the kitchen sees the order only once the webhook marks it paid. Tap "Simulate failure" on the payment screen to preview the recovery path.
